# Step 1 · Story interview

Interview the user before any image or video. Ask in blocks of 3 to 5 questions, show what you already understood, and only ask what is still open. Save the answers in `intent.md`; every answer becomes a field in `plan.json` or in the story bible.

When the user says "whatever", propose two concrete options and let them pick. Never invent the gag, the ending, brand colours or a character's personality.

The interview ends when no shot depends on a guess.

## Block 1 · The idea in one sentence
1. What is the story in one sentence, with a start, a turn and an end?
2. What should the viewer feel in the last second?
3. What is the gag or the turn, and at what second does it happen?
4. Who is it for and where does it play (9:16 Reel, 16:9 inside a Reel, YouTube)?
5. Final duration. Is there a brand, product or text at the end?

## Block 2 · Characters (one pass per character)
1. Age, size, how they walk and run.
2. What they want in the story and what gets in the way.
3. One habit with a trigger: "when X, they do Y".
4. One typical physical mistake: trips, drops things, holds things wrong.
5. A real person whose manner they resemble, to observe (never to copy their looks).
6. Their identity colour, which never changes.
7. Hands: confirm the finger count (five for humans) and hand size relative to the face.

## Block 3 · Place and objects
1. Which places appear; what sits left, right and behind in each.
2. Where the light comes from and the time of day.
3. Which objects the story uses, and who holds them with which hand.
4. Do water, steam, bubbles or fire appear? They are always drawn shapes.

## Block 4 · Action per moment
1. What the character does, with which object, how many times.
2. What they just saw or felt (the cause of the expression).
3. What is on screen in the last frame of the shot.
4. What triggers the cut: a look, an action, a sound, an object filling the frame.

## Block 5 · Sound and rhythm
1. Music: own, library or generated? Where does it enter and stop?
2. Dialogue? If none, write "no dialogue" explicitly: an open mouth tends to become a voice in video models.
3. Which contact sounds matter (paper, pop, splash)?

## Block 6 · References and limits
1. Which images or films are references, and for what exactly (line, colour, rhythm, acting). Technique only.
2. What must NOT appear.
3. Credit ceiling for this production.

## Closing
Read back a five-line summary and the shot list with durations, then ask: "Can I build the story bible?" Nothing is generated without that yes.

## Example (BOLHAO)
> A boy finds a magic bubble gum by a lake, blows a bubble that carries him over the water, falls in when it pops, and wakes up in bed to find it was a dream.

Last frame decided in the interview: his embarrassed sideways glance at a damp blanket. No dialogue. Music added in the edit.
