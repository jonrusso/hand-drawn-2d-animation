# Tyrus Wong (1910-2016)

This skill is dedicated to Tyrus Wong. Its look was studied from his work, and it would not exist without him.

## His life
- **Born** Wong Gen Yeo on 25 October 1910 in Taishan, Guangdong, China.
- **Arrived in California** at nine, with his father, in 1920. Under the Chinese Exclusion Act he was held at the Angel Island Immigration Station for about a month and entered as a "paper son" under another name. He became an American citizen only in 1946.
- **Studied** at the Otis Art Institute in Los Angeles on a scholarship that his teachers helped him win, then painted murals for the WPA during the Depression.
- **At Disney, 1938 to 1941,** he started as an in-betweener. Paintings he made at home of a deer in a forest set the visual style of *Bambi* (1942), and he became its lead artist, though the credits listed him only as one of several background artists. He left the studio after the 1941 animators' strike.
- **At Warner Bros., 1942 to 1968,** he worked as a production illustrator and storyboard artist on live-action films such as *Sands of Iwo Jima*, *Rebel Without a Cause* and *The Wild Bunch*.
- **Beyond film,** he designed Hallmark greeting cards, painted dinnerware, made lithographs and ceramics, and after retiring built and flew kites by the beach in Santa Monica into his last years.
- **Recognition came late.** Disney named him a Disney Legend in 2001. The Walt Disney Family Museum held a retrospective of his art in 2013. He died on 30 December 2016, aged 106.

## Why this skill follows him
His forests are not drawn leaf by leaf. Drawing on the landscape painting of the Song dynasty, he suggested a whole world with soft washes, a few sure strokes, light through mist, and room for the viewer's eye to finish the picture. Most AI animation fails the other way: too much detail, hard edges everywhere, nothing left to the imagination.

The look this skill asks for (painted atmosphere over detail, soft edges, light that belongs to the place) is an attempt to chase that feeling with new tools. The debt is his.

## How we keep this respectful
- His name and his films are never written into prompts; the prompt checker blocks them. The style is described in plain words instead.
- This repository contains none of his artwork and none of Disney's. Please do not add any.
- This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by his family, his estate or Disney.
